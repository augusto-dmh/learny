# Brazilian market for autonomous / self-directed study tools and AI-assisted learning (as of 2026-09-30)

Scope note: numbers below are from Brazilian government (INEP/MEC, Banco Central, ANPD), Cetic.br, company pages, and Brazilian tech/business press. Where a figure came only from a search-engine summary rather than a page I opened, it is marked "(search summary)". Estimates are labelled.

## Key Question 1: How many people in Brazil are in each autonomous-study segment per year, and how much do they pay today for study tools?

### Takeaway
Brazil's addressable self-study population is in the tens of millions: ~5.06M ENEM registrants (2026), ~9M concurso registrations/year in a >R$5bn market, 10.1M undergraduates (50.7% now in EAD), ~150k OAB candidates per sitting (3 sittings/yr), plus millions of tech upskillers and language learners. Paid study tools cluster at R$30–60/month for question banks and video cursinhos, R$99–229/month for premium concurso bundles, and ~R$87–170/month (annual-only) for tech upskilling.

### Inferences
- Segment sizing (estimate): ENEM ~5M/yr; concursos ~9M registrations/yr (many are repeat registrants, so unique concurseiros are likely 2–4M); undergraduates 10.1M (5.1M EAD, the most "autonomous" cohort); OAB ~150k x 3 sittings (~300–450k registrations/yr, overlapping); tech upskillers several million (Alura 6M cumulative, GitHub 6.9M); language learners tens of millions (Duolingo 70M cumulative downloads).
- Price ladder Brazilians already accept for study SaaS: R$15–25 (Duolingo Super, Spotify), R$30–60 (question banks, video cursinhos), R$99–229 (premium concurso bundles), R$87–170 annual-only (tech). A book-grounded study tool priced at R$19.90–39.90/month would sit in the "cheap utility" band; above R$59.90 it competes with content-rich bundles.
- Installment pricing ("12x R$X") and Pix/boleto discounts are the norm; Brazilian buyers evaluate on the monthly installment, not the annual total.

### Gaps
- No public unique-user count for concurseiros; the 9M figure counts registrations across many exams.
- Could not find 2025 registrant counts for the 44º/45º OAB exams (only 43º).
- No reliable per-segment "monthly spend on study tools" survey found; the R$5bn concurso figure includes travel and fees.
- DIO (Digital Innovation One) user figures were not found in this pass.

## Key Question 2: Which Brazilian products have added AI study features (with dates) and how well are they received?

### Takeaway
Nearly every large Brazilian edtech now bundles an AI assistant (Gran's MAIA since 2024, Descomplica's IA study assistant and essay corrector, Árvore.ai since Mar 2023, Geekie, Knowunity's SchoolGPT), but the AI is layered on the vendor's own catalogue, and the loudest learner complaints are about billing, auto-renewal, and cancellation, not AI quality.

### Cited Findings
- **Gran Cursos Online — MAIA**: launched at Gran's "Reinvenção 2024" as a study companion; generates subtitles, audiobook reading, course comparison ("Dá pra Conciliar"), recommendations, and explanatory comments on questions; the engine has produced >1.2M automated question explanations; in 2025 MAIA gained a 24-hour tutor — [Gran Zendesk: MAIA](https://grancursosonline.zendesk.com/hc/pt-br/articles/21798168734235-MAIA-a-Intelig%C3%AAncia-Artificial-do-Gran); [Gran blog: Revisão Inteligente](https://blog.grancursosonline.com.br/revisao-inteligente/); [Brazil Journal](https://braziljournal.com/brands/como-o-gran-vai-usar-a-ia-para-chegar-a-1-milhao-de-alunos-e-meio-bilhao-em-receita/)
- **Descomplica**: "assistente de estudos com IA" that "resume a aula, aprofunda o tópico e responde dúvida na hora", plus AI essay correction trained on ENEM's five competencies with feedback, 30 corrections/month, included in Enem, graduação and pós plans — [Descomplica ENEM page](https://descomplica.com.br/vestibulares/enem/)
- **Estratégia Concursos**: its own blog (5 May 2024) recommends third-party ChatGPT and Chatmind for summaries/flashcards/mind maps rather than an in-house AI product; search summaries mention AI-generated mind maps and adaptive schedules but I found no dated first-party launch announcement — [Estratégia blog](https://www.estrategiaconcursos.com.br/blog/inteligencia-artificial-ferramenta-estudo/)
- **Árvore (reading platform)**: launched Árvore.ai (10 Mar 2023), a ChatGPT-like layer over its 30k-book library that answers questions about the book, summarizes, and simplifies concepts; >1M students reached, 500+ publishers — [PublishNews](https://www.publishnews.com.br/materias/2023/03/10/arvore-lanca-plataforma-de-ia-para-expandir-experiencia-de-leitura-dos-usuarios); [Direcional Escolas](https://direcionalescolas.com.br/tecnologia-como-aliada-da-educacao-plataformas-da-arvore-levam-leitura-para-mais-de-1-milhao-de-alunos/)

### Inferences
- Incumbent AI is "AI over our catalogue" (Gran's questions, Descomplica's lessons, Árvore's licensed library). None found lets a learner upload *their own* book and get citation-anchored answers; Árvore.ai is the closest in spirit but is school-licensed and library-bound.
- Learner pain is concentrated in subscription hygiene (cancel, refund, auto-renew). A transparent monthly Pix plan with one-click cancel is itself a differentiator in this market.
- Venture funding for new Brazilian edtechs is scarce (2024: 7 new startups), which reduces well-funded local competition but also signals investor skepticism.

### Gaps
- No dated launch for a Qconcursos-branded AI feature was found; searches returned only AI-topic questions in its bank. Treat "Qconcursos AI" as unverified.
- No dated first-party Estratégia AI launch found.
- Google Play review sentiment on the AI features specifically (as opposed to billing) was not sampled.
- Redação Nota 1000, Eduqo, Kultivi, Poliedro AI features were not researched due to call budget.

## Key Question 3: What are the concrete constraints for launching a study SaaS in Brazil (payments, pricing, LGPD, device/network, language)?

### Takeaway
Brazil is mobile-only (60% overall, 86% in classes DE), Android-dominant (~75–83%), Pix-native (86% of adults), WhatsApp-saturated (~147M users), with a pricing ceiling anchored by Spotify (R$23.90) and Netflix (R$44.90). LGPD imposes special duties for minors (ENEM cohort), the AI bill PL 2338 is still unpassed, and Portuguese LLM quality is good but Brazilian-specialist models still trail frontier models on most benchmarks.

- ANPD published an *Enunciado* on processing children's and adolescents' data: any LGPD legal basis (consent, legal obligation, legitimate interest…) may apply, but the best interest of the child must prevail and be assessed by the controller — [ANPD](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-divulga-enunciado-sobre-o-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-de-criancas-e-adolescentes); [Lefosse](https://lefosse.com/noticias/alerta/anpd-publica-enunciado-sobre-o-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-de-criancas-e-adolescentes/)
- ANPD is drafting a "Guia Orientativo de Proteção de Dados Pessoais de Crianças e Adolescentes" with specific guidance for educational institutions; its four enforcement priorities include minors online and AI/emerging tech (Câmara hearing, 30 Sep 2025) — [ANPD presentation PDF](https://www2.camara.leg.br/atividade-legislativa/comissoes/grupos-de-trabalho/57a-legislatura/grupo-de-trabalho-sobre-protecao-de-criancas-e-adolescentes-em-ambiente-digital/apresentacoes-em-eventos/25.09.30RODRIGOSANTANADOSSANTOSCoordenadorGeraldeNormatizaodaANPD.pdf); [Participa + Brasil](https://www.gov.br/participamaisbrasil/tscriancaeadolescente)
- MEC published a "Referencial para o Uso e Desenvolvimento Responsáveis de IA na Educação" and opened a regulatory sandbox (2025): four risk tiers; organizing materials/accessibility = low risk; automatic test grading and biometric monitoring = high risk; AI banned from pass/fail decisions; requires human oversight, transparency, data protection — [Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/novas-iniciativas-mec-inteligencia-artificial/); [Jeduca](https://jeduca.org.br/noticia/ia-na-educacao-entenda-o-novo-referencial-do-mec-e-pontos-de-atencao); [MEC consulta pública, Oct 2025](https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2025/outubro/mec-abrira-consulta-publica-sobre-ia-na-educacao); [MEC orientador PDF](https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/escolas-conectadas/documentos/ia-educacao-basica.pdf)
- PL 2338/2023 (Marco Legal da IA): passed Senate 10 Dec 2024; in the Câmara special committee (rapporteur Aguinaldo Ribeiro) with a report in May 2026; not yet law as of Sep 2026; open points include copyright and high-risk exceptions; fines up to R$50M cited — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/16/12/2025/pl-de-inteligencia-artificial-fica-para-fevereiro-de-2026/); [Grownt](https://grownt.tech/pl-da-ia-avanca-na-camara-mas-redata-e-impasse-com-o-senado-travam-o-calendario/); [Entercast](https://www.entercastconsulting.com.br/blog/marco-legal-ia-brasil-votacao-camara-maio-2026)

**Copyright for uploaded books**
- Lei 9.610/98 art. 46, II permits reproduction "em um só exemplar de pequenos trechos, para uso privado do copista, desde que feita por este, sem intuito de lucro"; "pequeno trecho" is undefined (courts have cited 10–30%, 10% most common) — [Jusbrasil art. 46](https://www.jusbrasil.com.br/topicos/10625543/artigo-46-da-lei-n-9610-de-19-de-fevereiro-de-1998); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/coluna/pi-migalhas/196781/reproducao-parcial-e-usos-livres)
- ABDR: book piracy losses of R$1.2bn in 2024; ABDR/SNEL found 15,404 full-book pirate links in one month (+56.4% MoM); hosts included Chomikuj (51%), Scribd (25%), 4Shared (15%), Passei Direto (2%), plus Mercado Livre, Docsity, Shopee; 36% of pirated titles are scientific/technical/professional — [PublishNews](https://www.publishnews.com.br/materias/2025/02/12/pirataria-de-livros-gera-prejuizo-de-r-12-bilhao-em-2024-e-abdr-intensifica-acoes); [SNEL](https://snel.org.br/pirataria-digital-segue-como-desafio-para-o-mercado-editorial/)

**Portuguese-language LLM quality**
- Maritaca Sabiá-3 (Oct 2024 tech report): accuracy comparable to GPT-4o across 64 Brazilian exams (OAB, ENEM, ENADE…); evaluated on 73 national exams, long-context (Dom Casmurro needle test up to 32k tokens), IFEval 78.0 loose / 73.8 strict — [arXiv 2410.12049](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12049); [Maritaca research](https://www.maritaca.ai/en/research/)
- Sabiá-4 preview (19 Jan 2026), 128k context: OAB-Bench 7.49 (GPT-4.1 7.30; Gemini-3-Pro 8.90); Leis Brasileiras 97.4 (GPT-4.1 80.8; Gemini-3-Pro 88.6); Multi-IF Português 82.0 (GPT-4.1 82.7; Gemini-3-Pro 88.0); agentic 72.2 (GPT-4.1 73.3; Gemini-3-Pro 90.1); full benchmark run cost R$80.49 — [Maritaca Sabiá-4](https://www.maritaca.ai/post/sabia-4); Sabiazinho-4 preview 6 Jan 2026 and Sabiá 4 Thinking (Jun 2026) — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/inteligencia-artificial/sabiazinho-4-brasileira-maritaca-lanca-novo-modelo-de-ia/); [SergipeWeb](https://sergipeweb.com.br/maritaca-ai-lanca-sabia-4-thinking-modelo-que-compete/)

### Inferences
- Design constraints: PWA/Android-first, small payloads, offline reading cache, and low-end device support are not optional for ENEM and classes C/D/E learners.
- Pricing: a "Spotify-band" price (R$19.90–29.90/month) with Pix and a student tier mirrors what Brazilians already pay; annual plans need 12x installments to convert.
- Legal: Learny's use case (a learner uploads a book they own and gets extracts/citations for private study) maps to art. 46 private-use logic if the tool never redistributes files between users; a user-to-user sharing feature would move it into ABDR's crosshairs (Passei Direto is already on their list).
- LGPD: ENEM users include 15–17-year-olds; a minors flow (parental consent or documented legitimate-interest assessment, data minimization, no ad profiling) is required, and MEC's framework favors "organizing materials" (low risk) over grading (high risk), which suits a citation/teaching tool.
- Model choice: frontier models (Gemini 3 Pro, GPT-5.x) still lead Sabiá-4 on most pt-BR benchmarks, so ADR-0020's Anthropic default is defensible; Sabiá is a cost/latency and data-residency option, not a quality necessity.

### Gaps
- No ANPD guidance specifically about *storing uploaded copyrighted books* was found; the guidance covers personal data, not content licensing.
- No Brazilian benchmark comparing Claude models on pt-BR exams was found in this pass (Maritaca's tables list GPT and Gemini).
- No data on typical mobile data plan sizes for students or on app-size sensitivity.

## Key Question 4: Where is the white space: which needs are unmet by both global players (NotebookLM, ChatGPT) and local players, for a Portuguese-speaking autonomous learner?

### Takeaway
Brazilian learners already use AI massively (Brazil is the #3 ChatGPT market; 70% of high-schoolers use generative AI for schoolwork; NotebookLM is localized), but no product found combines the learner's *own* books, structure-preserving reading, verifiable citations, teaching and review, in Portuguese, on a Pix-priced plan; incumbents lock AI to their catalogues and global tools are generic and unpinned to a study routine.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI "Desbloqueando Oportunidades Econômicas para o Brasil" (12 Aug 2025): Brazil is #3 in weekly ChatGPT usage after the US and India, ~140M messages/day; 27% of users are 18–24 and 33% are 25–34; top uses: writing/communication 20%, learning 15%, math/data/programming 6%; Brazil is #2 in developers using the OpenAI API — [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2025/08/12/pro/top-3-brasil-e-um-dos-paises-que-mais-usa-chatgpt-no-mundo-veja-as-principais-utilidades/); [Softex](https://softex.br/brasil-e-3o-maior-usuario-global-do-chatgpt-segundo-relatorio-da-openai/); [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/inteligencia-artificial/brasil-esta-entre-os-3-paises-que-mais-usam-chatgpt-diz-openai/)
- TIC Educação 2024 (fieldwork Aug 2024–Mar 2025, 10,756 interviews in 1,023 schools): 70% of high-school students use generative AI for school research; 39% in final elementary years, 15% in early years; only 32% received school guidance on AI use; 74% use search engines and 72% video apps as information sources — [Cetic.br](https://cetic.br/noticia/sete-em-cada-dez-alunos-do-ensino-medio-usam-ia-generativa-em-pesquisas-escolares-revela-tic-educacao/); [Porvir](https://porvir.org/muita-ia-pouca-mediacao-cenario-tic-2024/); [Cetic PDF](https://cetic.br/media/analises/tic_educacao_2024_principais_resultados.pdf)
- NotebookLM works in Brazil with pt-BR Audio Overviews (Google blog), mobile apps, free tier "complete enough" for studying, flashcards and quizzes; Brazilian teachers report one notebook per subject — [Google Brasil blog](https://blog.google/intl/pt-br/produtos/notebooklm-agora-oferece-resumos-em-audio-em-portugues-brasileiro/); [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/notebooklm-ferramenta-de-anotacoes-com-ia-do-google-ja-funciona-no-brasil/); [Prof. Edigley Alexandre, Sep 2025](https://www.prof-edigleyalexandre.com/2025/09/notebooklm-o-que-e-como-usar-no-brasil-exemplos-praticos-e-algumas-dicas.html); [Rocketseat blog](https://www.rocketseat.com.br/blog/artigos/post/como-aprender-com-notebooklm-google)
- Google's 2025 "Year in Search" Brasil listed Gemini, DeepSeek, Veo 3, NotebookLM and Grok among top queries — [Esmael Morais](https://www.esmaelmorais.com.br/google-pesquisas-2025)
- Google's student offer bundles "notebooks de estudo" that turn class materials into guides and quizzes (search summary of Canaltech) — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/inteligencia-artificial/google-ai-pro-gemini-de-graca-por-1-ano-brasil/)
- Local incumbents' AI is catalogue-bound: Gran MAIA (Gran questions/courses), Descomplica assistant (its lessons), Árvore.ai (its licensed library) — sources in KQ2.
- Small local "upload PDF → flashcards" apps (estudAe, StudyFi, Estuda IA, Thea) exist but position as flashcard/quiz generators, not as cited reading + teaching — sources in KQ2.
- Reading decline context: CNN Brasil piece on why students stop reading and how schools form readers — [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/educacao/por-que-os-alunos-deixam-de-ler-e-como-a-escola-pode-formar-leitores/)
- MEC framework rates "organizing materials" as low-risk and demands transparency/explainability — [Jeduca](https://jeduca.org.br/noticia/ia-na-educacao-entenda-o-novo-referencial-do-mec-e-pontos-de-atencao)

### Inferences
- Demand is proven (140M ChatGPT messages/day; 70% of high-schoolers use GenAI) but unmediated (68% get no guidance), and Brazilian sources are video-first (72% use video apps as information sources): a product that teaches *from the book the learner already has*, with visible citations, addresses the trust gap that both ChatGPT (hallucination) and incumbents (catalogue lock-in) leave open.
- Unmet needs I could not find any product addressing in Portuguese: (1) bring-your-own EPUB/PDF with preserved chapter structure and page-anchored citations; (2) a single place that spans reading → cited Q&A → taught passages → spaced review across *all* the learner's sources (edital PDFs, law codes, textbooks); (3) transparent Pix monthly pricing with easy cancel; (4) minors-safe LGPD posture designed in.
- Segments where "own materials" matter most: concurseiros (editais, lei seca, apostilas as PDFs), OAB (Vade Mecum, doutrina), EAD undergraduates (institution PDFs), tech upskillers (English technical books needing pt-BR teaching).
- Global players' weaknesses in Brazil: NotebookLM's free tier is strong, so Learny cannot win on "chat with PDF"; it must win on structure, citation verifiability, teaching pedagogy and local pricing/payments.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends time series was retrieved (Trends pages are not fetchable via search); rely on Google's Year-in-Search mention and Portugal's +90% GenAI interest as directional only.
- No Reddit (r/concursos, r/enem, r/brasil) threads were surfaced by search; demand evidence from those communities is missing.
- No NotebookLM pt-BR user counts were found; Google has not published country figures.

## Key Question 5: What Portuguese-language content sources and communities could feed adoption?

### Takeaway
Free legal corpora exist (Portal Domínio Público >123k works, MEC Livros 1,700 titles, Project Gutenberg's Portuguese shelf), incumbents give away massive free video/question funnels, and distribution runs through WhatsApp groups/channels, YouTube, TikTok, and a fast-growing dev community (6.9M GitHub users, TabNews, Rocketseat Discord 240k).

### Cited Findings
- Portal Domínio Público (MEC, since 2004): >123k free works including Machado de Assis and José de Alencar — [TechTudo](https://www.techtudo.com.br/listas/2026/04/sites-para-ler-livros-gratis-veja-opcoes-seguras-alem-do-mec-livros-edsoftwares.ghtml); [Mettzer](https://blog.mettzer.com/dominio-publico/)
- MEC Livros – "A Biblioteca Digital do Brasil": 1,700 public-domain works — [MEC Livros](https://meclivros.app.br/); [UFRB](https://www.ufrb.edu.br/cfp/atividades-para-a-comunidade/4203-mec-livros-a-nova-biblioteca-digital-gratuita-do-brasil)

- Árvore: 30k-book licensed library with 500+ publishers (B2B school model) — [PublishNews](https://www.publishnews.com.br/materias/2023/03/10/arvore-lanca-plataforma-de-ia-para-expandir-experiencia-de-leitura-dos-usuarios)

### Inferences
- A pre-loaded "biblioteca gratuita" (Domínio Público, MEC Livros, Gutenberg pt, public-domain law texts from planalto.gov.br) gives first-run value without copyright risk and serves ENEM literature lists (Machado, Alencar) directly.
- Open-sourcing Learny (already on GitHub) fits the Brazilian dev community's preference for open, self-hostable tools; TabNews launch posts and Rocketseat/Discord communities are low-cost distribution for the tech-upskiller segment.
- WhatsApp share links of cited passages and a Canal for daily "passagem do dia" fit the dominant channel; study-group leaders are natural multipliers.
